## Notes

- Growing window: 6 months ending at quarter end for every crop and quarter (`GROWING_WINDOW_MONTHS` in `src/cropyield/features.py`). Only imagery ending on or before quarter end is used.
- Combined PSA reporting units use their members' VI rows weighted by valid pixels; a date is missing unless every member is valid.
- Satellite features are missing before each source starts (MODIS 2001, Sentinel-2 2017) and when a window has too few valid observations.
- Rainfall (Open-Meteo ERA5, daily totals at one cropland point per province, summed to months) columns `rain_*` use the calendar months of the same window; a missing month makes the aggregate missing. `rain_anom` is relative to the fixed 1981-2000 climatology.
